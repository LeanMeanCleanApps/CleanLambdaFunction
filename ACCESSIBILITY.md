# Accessibility

Everyone should be able to read the docs, use the templates and take part here, whether that's with a mouse, a keyboard, a screen reader, a magnifier or a strong coffee and squinting.

The full statement lives at [leanmeanclean.dev/accessibility](https://leanmeanclean.dev/accessibility/). It covers what we aim for (WCAG 2.2 AA in everything we build), the third-party tools the templates ship but we don't build, what we know isn't right yet, and what happens after you tell us.

## Found a barrier?

[Report an accessibility barrier](https://github.com/LeanMeanCleanApps/leanmeancleanapps.github.io/issues/new?template=accessibility_barrier.yml). Tell us where it was and what got in the way. You don't need to quote WCAG at us or tell us anything about yourself, and you'll hear back within 7 days.

If it's in Scalar, Nitro, MCP Inspector, Open WebUI or the Aspire dashboard, tell us anyway. We'll pass it on to the people who can fix it, and find you a workaround in the meantime.
